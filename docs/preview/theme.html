Flag black surfaces, yellow-green as the primary accent, gold for measured values, flag green for confirmed state. Dark is the only scheme: an AR viewfinder with white chrome halves the contrast of the camera image and burns battery on OLED, and the operator's eyes are adapted to a dim room.
