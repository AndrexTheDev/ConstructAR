How accurate is it?

Stated plainly, because you will bill from it.

SituationExpected deviation
Flat wall, 1–4 m, detected plane±2 cm
Areas with depth only±6 cm

Runs are measured between anchor-refined surface points, never on the offset centreline. Anything critical should still be checked with a tape measure — the protocol says so too.

Questions from the site

Does it work without internet?

Yes — the app has no INTERNET permission at all. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is downloaded, and tracking, depth and rendering run on the phone.

Which phones are supported?

Android phones with ARCore and Android 7.0 or newer. A depth sensor (ToF) improves the numbers but is not required.

Is it a laser replacement?

For runs on flat walls, yes, to within a couple of centimetres. For anything technical or hidden behind a panel, check with a tape measure before cutting — the protocol states the expected error too.

Where do my measurements live?

In one JSON file per room, in the app's private storage. You can copy every project and its photo into a folder of your choice and restore them on another phone.

Do I need an account?

No. No sign-in, no cloud, no subscription tracking — a licence is a short signed key that the app verifies offline.

How do I pay?

In SOL, BTC or ETH to the address shown in the app. Send the transaction id and your device code, and you get a licence key back.
